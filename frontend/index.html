008 / FAQ
Q.001 / What is Cipher?+

Cipher is an AI-powered investigative intelligence platform designed to connect fragmented case information into a visual, searchable relationship graph.

Q.002 / Is Cipher a replacement for investigators?+

No. Cipher is designed as an analytical assistant. Investigators remain responsible for validating evidence, interpreting context and making decisions.

Q.003 / What makes the interface different?+

The product centers the investigation around relationships and evidence rather than forcing users to jump between disconnected dashboards.

Q.004 / Can the design be adapted?+

Yes. Cipher is designed as a focused intelligence interface: simple enough to use quickly, but rich enough to reveal complex relationships.
